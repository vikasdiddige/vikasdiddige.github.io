Teaching activity

Introduction to the finite element method

Introduction to FEM principles for linear solid-mechanics problems: strong and weak forms, discretization, shape functions, assembly, and boundary conditions.

Exercises use SIMULIA Abaqus FEA for strength-of-materials problems.

Teaching activity

Nonlinear finite element methods

Application of FEM to physically nonlinear boundary-value problems, including introductory thermo-mechanical coupling and dynamical problems.
