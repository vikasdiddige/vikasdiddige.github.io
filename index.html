07 / Teaching

Teaching finite element methods.

Exercise instruction in core and non-linear finite element methods.

Teaching activity

Introduction to the finite element method

The course gives a concise introduction to the fundamental principles of the Finite Element Method, with particular application to linear partial differential equations relevant in solid mechanics.

It covers strong and weak forms of equilibrium equations, spatial discretization and shape functions, assembly operations, and boundary conditions. The method is applied to one- and two-dimensional quasistatic boundary-value problems, with an outlook on physically non-linear problems.

Exercises use SIMULIA Abaqus FEA to obtain approximate solutions to well-known strength-of-materials problems.

Teaching activity

Non-linear finite element methods

The course focuses on applying the Finite Element Method to physically non-linear boundary-value problems.

It also includes introductory lectures on thermo-mechanical coupling and discusses the application of the FEM to dynamical problems.
